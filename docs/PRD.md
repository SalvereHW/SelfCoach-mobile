# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Salvere Holistic Wellness - SelfCoach (MVP)

Document Version: 1.0 Date: October 26, 2023 Prepared For: Engineering Team

## 1. Introduction & Purpose

The Salvere Holistic wellness - SelfCoach app aims to "Help users achieve holistic wellness and manage specific health conditions with ease and affordability". This PRD outlines the core features for the app's Minimum Viable Product (MVP), prioritizing essential functionalities that address primary user needs and key differentiators. The goal is to deliver a focused initial product that proves market value before expanding.

## 2. Target Audience

The primary users for Salvere's MVP include individuals experiencing:
Specific health conditions such as ADHD, sleep disorders, and hypertension.
Low Sleep Quality.
Intense Daily Routine and difficulty integrating wellness.
Forgetfulness regarding health activities.
Needs for Personalized Diet Plans that respect Cultural Diet preferences.
Users seeking Convenient Access to wellness management via mobile devices.
Individuals sensitive to Cost of health solutions.

## 3. Problem Statement (Jobs To Be Done - JTBD)

Users currently struggle with:
Finding Personalized Plans that cater to diverse and specific health issues.
Comprehensive Tracking of various health metrics in one place.
User Engagement and consistent adherence to health plans.
Integrating wellness into an Intense Daily Routine.
Forgetfulness leading to inconsistency in health practices.
The Cost of premium health features and services.
Lack of Convenient Access to manage wellness from mobile devices.

## 4. Solution Overview (MVP)

The Salvere SelfCoach app (MVP) will be a mobile-first, holistic health application focused on delivering personalized health management for a select set of conditions, coupled with essential engagement tools and a cost-effective pricing model. It will initially provide "more tailored health plans compared to generic fitness and wellness apps" and aim for a "Lower subscription cost compared to many competitors". The app is positioned within "Software Services - Low Regulatory Bar" and "Public Health Services", focusing on user engagement and data privacy rather than extensive clinical validation.

## 5. Prioritized Features (MVP Focus)

The following features represent the core MVP functionalities:

### 5.1. Core Personalized Health Management & Integrated Tracking

Goal: To provide highly personalized support for key conditions and enable essential self-tracking. This directly addresses the "Personalized Plans" and "Comprehensive Tracking" "Reasons For Hire."
Feature: Personalized Health Plans (Initial Focus: Sleep, Hypertension, ADHD)
Description: The app will offer custom health plans for specific, initial conditions such as ADHD, sleep disorders, and hypertension. For users identifying with "Low Sleep Quality", this will include:
Sleep Tracking to monitor and analyze sleep patterns.
Personalized Sleep Improvement Tips and exercises.
Guided Relaxation and Sleep Meditation sessions to help users wind down.
Rationale: Directly addresses a core "Reason For Hire" and helps "Improve Mental Health" and "Promote Healthy Lifestyles".
Feature: Basic Integrated Tracking (Nutrition, Sleep, Activity)
Description: Users will be able to track basic nutrition, sleep, and activity metrics within the app.
For Nutrition: "Personalized Diet Plans" that "respect and incorporate cultural dietary preferences" will be developed. This includes culturally appropriate healthy recipe suggestions and nutrition tips aligned with various cultural diets.
For Sleep: As mentioned above, basic sleep pattern tracking.
For Activity: Simple logging of fitness activities.
Rationale: Provides "Comprehensive Tracking", addressing a key user need, and specifically caters to the "Cultural Diet" challenge.

### 5.2. Essential User Engagement & Seamless Routine Integration

Goal: To ensure consistent user engagement and help users integrate wellness into busy lives. This tackles the "User Engagement" "Barriers To Hire" and addresses "Forgetfulness" and "Intense Daily Routine".
Feature: Smart Reminders & Health Prompts
Description: Implement customizable and intelligent reminders that "adapt to the user's schedule and preferences". This includes:
Smart Reminders for medication, appointments, or plan activities.
Health Prompts for hydration, stretching, and other wellness activities throughout the day.
Rationale: Directly combats "Forgetfulness" and promotes consistent adherence.
Feature: Productivity Management & Quick Wellness Sessions
Description: Develop features that help users organize and optimize their daily routines, seamlessly integrating wellness activities. This includes:
Offering short, effective Quick Workouts and Meditation sessions that "fit into tight schedules".
Providing basic Time Management Tips to help users balance their busy lives with health and wellness.
Rationale: Addresses the "Intense Daily Routine" barrier by making wellness accessible and manageable within busy schedules.

### 5.3. Affordability & Convenient Mobile Access

Goal: To make holistic wellness accessible and convenient for a broad user base, leveraging the app's strategic positioning. This addresses "Cost" as a "Barrier To Hire" and "Convenient Access" as a "Reason For Hire."

- Feature: Mobile-First Platform
Description: The app will be designed primarily for mobile devices, allowing users to "manage wellness from mobile devices" with ease.
Rationale: "Convenient Access" is a core "Reason For Hire" and essential for broad adoption.

- Feature: Clear & Competitive Pricing Model
Description: Implement a clear pricing structure with a lower subscription cost for premium features compared to many competitors. This includes a well-defined free tier and premium options.
Rationale: Directly addresses "Cost" as a "Barriers To Hire" and offers "Affordable Premium Features" as a key improvement over existing alternatives. The app's positioning in "Software Services - Low Regulatory Bar" and "Public Health Services" also supports this affordability.

- Feature: Basic Financial Responsibility Tools (Cost-Saving Tips)
Description: Provide cost-saving tips and strategies to save money on healthy eating and fitness activities. This will be focused on informational tips rather than complex financial integrations for the MVP.
Rationale: Helps users manage health-related expenses and mitigates the "Cost" barrier.

## 6. Non-Prioritized Features (Out of MVP Scope)

To maintain a lean MVP, the following features, while valuable, will be considered for future phases:
Extensive Community Forums & Fitness Buddies: While "Supportive Community" is a "Reason For Hire", full-fledged community features like "Community Forums", "Fitness Buddies", and "Family and Group Subscriptions" will be deferred to later stages to simplify initial development and moderation.
Deep Wearable Technology Integration: While "Wearable Technology Integration" is a "Tool Hired", complex or extensive integrations with external wearable devices will be added post-MVP to avoid "Technical Complexity". Basic manual input or limited integrations will be supported first.
Health and Wellness Practitioner Tools: Features for "Health and Wellness Practitioners" such as "Integration with Common Tools", "Centralized Platform", "Unified Dashboard", "Client Testimonials", and "Marketing Tools" are considered a separate product or a later phase of expansion for professionals, not the core user MVP.
Insurance Integration: While beneficial for "Financial Responsibility", detailed "Insurance Integration" will be deferred to a later phase to simplify the initial scope.

## 7. High-Level Technical Requirements

Platform: Native mobile applications (iOS and Android).
Backend: Scalable cloud-based infrastructure to support personalized data and user growth.
Data Privacy & Security: Critical adherence to data privacy standards and security protocols from the outset to address "Privacy Concerns".
API Design: APIs for content delivery (personalized plans, tips, recipes) and data collection (tracking metrics).

## 8. Metrics for Success (MVP)

User Engagement: Daily Active Users (DAU) and Weekly Active Users (WAU) to measure "consistent user engagement and adherence to plans".
Feature Adoption: Usage rates for personalized plans, tracking features, reminders, and quick sessions.
User Satisfaction: Feedback on the app's ease of use and the value of personalized content.
Premium Feature Conversion (if applicable): Rate at which users convert from free to premium subscriptions.

## 9. Regulatory & Compliance

The SelfCoach app is positioned within "Software Services - Low Regulatory Bar" and "Public Health Services".
Rationale: The app "does not make any direct clinical claims or provide diagnostic tools that affect clinical decision-making". It offers "general wellness and health tracking features, similar to other low-regulated wellness apps and services".
Timeline: Regulatory submission is anticipated around Month 12, focusing on "documentation of the app's functionalities, user safety measures, data privacy protocols, and compliance with relevant health app standards".
